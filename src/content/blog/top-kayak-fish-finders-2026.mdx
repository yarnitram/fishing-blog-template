## 💡 3 Golden Rules of Kayak Electronics Rigging

1. **Use Waterproof Connectors:** Salt spray and freshwater splash will corrode exposed wiring within weeks. Use heat-shrink tubing with adhesive lining.
2. **Fuse Your Setup:** Always place an inline 3A or 5A fuse near the positive terminal of your battery.
3. **Keep the Battery Dry:** Store your battery inside a sealed waterproof drybox or dedicated battery hatch bag.

Tight lines, and safe rigging on the water!

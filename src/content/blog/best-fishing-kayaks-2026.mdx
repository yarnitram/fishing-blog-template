## Buyer's Guide: What to Look For Before Buying

1. **Pedal vs. Paddle:** Pedal drives free your hands to hold the rod and constantly adjust your drift. However, paddle kayaks are lighter, simpler, and significantly cheaper.
2. **Hull Weight & Transport:** Consider how you will move your kayak. If you cannot back a trailer to the water, choose either a lighter hull or invest in a heavy-duty cart.
3. **Seat Comfort:** A bad seat ruins a morning on the water. Look for elevated aluminum frame stadium seats with breathable mesh.

## Conclusion

If your budget allows, the **Old Town Sportsman PDL 106** provides the most complete, hassle-free angling platform available today. If you want simplicity and portability, the **Perception Pescador Pro 10.0** is impossible to beat for under \$1,000.
